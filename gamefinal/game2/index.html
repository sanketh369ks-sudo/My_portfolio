📖 How to Play & Rules

1. Match Setup: Choose your squad and team strategy. Perform the coin toss to decide Batting or Bowling first.

2. Batting Mechanics: Select shots based on risk vs reward. Defend (Safe 0-1 run), Single (Placement), Lofted Shot (2-4 runs), Power Six (6 runs / Wicket risk), Scoop (High variance).

3. Bowling Mechanics: When bowling, choose deliveries like Yorker, Fast Pace, Bouncer, Spin, or Slower Ball to trick the computer batsman.

4. Victory Condition: In the 2nd innings, score more runs than the target to win by wickets, or restrict the opponent before 20 overs run out!
